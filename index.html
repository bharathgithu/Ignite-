About the organizer

I Hub School of Learning

I Hub School of Learning is a next-generation technology education platform committed to developing skilled professionals in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, and advanced computing.

Operating under the technology collaboration of NVIDIA, IHSL provides students with access to advanced AI platforms, GPU-powered computing environments, and globally recognized industry tools — including India's first NVIDIA Isaac Sim Lab.

  • Industry-oriented curriculum aligned with real AI & Robotics job roles
  • Under NVIDIA ecosystem — GPU-based AI platforms & tools
  • 100% hands-on training with live projects and hardware integration
  • 1-month industry internship with real corporate workflow experience
  • 100% placement assistance, mentorship & portfolio building
  • Certified by NSDC, AICRA, and Skill India
